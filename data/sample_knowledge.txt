Company Policy (Sample)

1) PTO:
Employees receive 15 days PTO per year. PTO requests should be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

2) Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. More than 2 days requires manager approval.

3) Security:
Do not paste secrets (API keys, passwords) into chat tools. Use environment variables or secret managers.

4) Expenses:
Reimbursements require receipts. Submit within 30 days of purchase.

FAQ:
- If a policy is not listed here, it is not approved by default.
